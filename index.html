PlayStation 策略分析：從硬體到平台的獲利轉型 ｜ PlayStation Strategic Analysis: The Platform-Driven Profit Transformation

Summer 2026
  • 本專案以 Sony 歷年財報與營運資料為基礎，分析 PlayStation 如何從硬體銷售逐步轉向以數位服務與平台變現為核心的商業模式。透過營收結構、數位下載比例、ARPU 與第一方軟體市佔率等指標，我進一步拆解全面數位化、平台變現與產品生命週期管理三項策略，並針對未來 3 至 5 年提出策略推演。
  • This project uses Sony’s historical financial and operating data to analyze PlayStation’s shift from a hardware-driven business toward a platform-centered model built on digital services and monetization. I examined indicators including revenue mix, digital download penetration, ARPU, and first-party software share to evaluate three strategic themes: digitalization, platform monetization, and product lifecycle management. The analysis concludes with strategic implications for PlayStation’s next 3–5 years.

臺灣鳳梨產業概況與交易價量分析 ｜ Taiwan Pineapple Industry Overview and Transaction Analysis

Spring 2026
  • 本報告整合農業部、批發市場與貿易資料，分析臺灣鳳梨的生產集中度、季節性供需、價格與進出口結構。研究發現主要產區高度集中於南部，並進一步探討 2021 年後出口市場由中國轉向日本所帶來的供應鏈與冷鏈挑戰，呈現臺灣鳳梨產業從規模導向逐步轉向高價值與高品質市場的發展趨勢。
  • This report integrates production, wholesale-market, and trade data to examine Taiwan’s pineapple industry through regional concentration, seasonal supply and demand, pricing, and international trade. The analysis highlights the industry’s geographic concentration in southern Taiwan and explores how the post-2021 shift from China toward Japan reshaped export strategy and cold-chain requirements. The findings illustrate Taiwan’s transition toward a more value- and quality-driven agricultural supply chain.

巴拉圭農業發展：大宗商品榮景與政策建議 ｜ Agricultural Development in Paraguay: Commodity Boom and Policy Recommendations

Spring 2026
  • 本報告從生產力、土地結構與氣候風險三個面向分析巴拉圭農業的長期轉型。研究發現，大豆與牛肉出口雖帶動生產效率提升，但高度集中的土地分配與對氣候的敏感度也增加了產業與總體經濟風險。基於分析結果，我進一步提出土地產權正式化、氣候韌性基礎建設與作物多元化等政策建議。
  • This report examines Paraguay’s agricultural transformation through productivity growth, land structure, and climate vulnerability. While soybean and beef exports have supported strong gains in agricultural efficiency, the analysis also identifies structural risks arising from concentrated land ownership and high exposure to climate shocks. Based on these findings, I developed policy recommendations focused on land-tenure formalization, climate-resilient infrastructure, and crop diversification.

標普500行業子指數的指數增強策略 ｜ Enhanced Index Strategy Based on S&P 500 Sub-Index

Winter 2026
  • 本專案探討總體經濟指標與標普 500 各行業子指數之間的連動關係，並以此建立指數增強策略。研究透過 CPI、聯邦基金利率與美元指數等總體因子，分析不同產業對經濟環境變化的敏感度，並結合歷史相關係數與標準化因子建立產業評分機制。進一步比較不同的資產配置與權重方法，以篩選在不同總體經濟環境下具備相對優勢的產業組合，建立動態的行業輪動策略。
  • This project investigates how macroeconomic indicators relate to S&P 500 industry sub-indices and uses those relationships to develop an enhanced index strategy. I analyzed sector sensitivity to changes in the Consumer Price Index, Federal Funds Rate, and U.S. Dollar Index, then combined historical correlations with standardized macro factors to build a sector-scoring framework. I compared several allocation and weighting methods to identify sectors that may be better positioned across different macroeconomic environments and implement systematic sector rotation.
